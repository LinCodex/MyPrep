# FAA figure-practice coverage

The bank now contains **660 original authored questions**, including **69 questions using 33 authentic FAA testing-supplement figures**. The initial 600 questions and their IDs remain unchanged; the 60 additions have new IDs. Adaptive calculation variants can increase the total further.

| Skill area | Figure questions | Practice covered |
|---|---:|---|
| Weather | 16 | METAR visibility/ceiling/wind/RVR; PIREP layers; TAF timing and changes; winds aloft decoding; SIGWX legends |
| Navigation | 14 | VOR selected courses and TO/FROM; compass cards; sectional airspace/elevation/obstruction data; Chart Supplement declared distances; UTC conversion |
| Performance | 13 | Pressure-altitude conversion; cruise tables; crosswind graphs; takeoff and landing graphs; assumptions and wind/grass corrections |
| Weight and balance | 9 | Station limits; loading tables and moment scales; loaded CG; forward/aft moment limits; normal/utility envelopes |
| Flight instruments | 8 | Altimeter hands and pressure settings; airspeed reading and limits; turn coordinator; attitude-indicator references |
| Airports | 8 | Taxi controls; VASI; closed/displaced runway depictions; windsocks; critical-area markings; airport signs |
| Aerodynamics | 1 | Load factor versus bank angle |
| **Total** | **69** | **33 distinct figures** |

The shared [FAA-CT-8080-2H supplement](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/supplements) also contains material for other certificates and aircraft, including helicopters, gyroplanes, gliders, and balloons. Including every illustration would not be an appropriate measure of FAA airplane PAR preparation.

Coverage was assessed against airplane knowledge skills and the [Private Pilot ACS](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf), rather than a purported fixed figure-question count on the live exam. The FAA does not publish its live question bank. This is representative coverage, not a claim to reproduce every possible examination question.

Use **Categories → Practice figures** for a mixed 20-question session, or select **Figure questions** in a category's custom filter. Images include their source figure number and can be enlarged, zoomed, and scrolled. Historical charts and weather examples are for training, not current flight-planning data.

Answers were checked against the actual rendered figures and handbook decoding/interpretation rules. Calculations were recomputed from the stated source data. This is source-based checking, not an aviation instructor's independent certification of the full bank.
